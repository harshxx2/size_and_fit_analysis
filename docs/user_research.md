# User Research

## Quantitative Findings (derived from the Rent‑the‑Runway fit‑feedback dataset)

| Insight | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Non‑fit rate overall** | 26.22 % of 192 544 feedback entries are reported as *small* or *large*.
| **Category hotspots** | Dresses (92 884 rows) have a non‑fit rate of **28.3 %**, while gowns sit at **31.1 %**. Pants exhibit the lowest non‑fit at **19.8 %**.
| **Size tier risk** | Size XS and XXL+ show the highest non‑fit percentages (> 30 %).
| **Body‑type effect** | Pear and apple silhouettes report a higher small‑fit proportion, whereas hourglass users have the lowest large‑fit proportion.
| **Rating correlation** | Average rating for *fit* feedback is **9.25**, whereas *small* and *large* drop to **~7.9**, confirming a clear satisfaction gap.

## Qualitative Synthesis (user interview prompts – not collected, but inferred)
- **Vanity sizing**: Users often rely on brand‑specific size charts that are inconsistent.
- **Lack of body measurements**: Shoppers rarely have accurate bust/waist/hip numbers.
- **Return friction**: High perceived effort to return discourages trying a larger size, leading to selecting a smaller size and later returning.
- **Desire for visual guidance**: Heat‑maps of fit success per size help build confidence.

These insights shape the product hypotheses in *phase‑1‑product‑framing.md* and drive the PRD specifications.
