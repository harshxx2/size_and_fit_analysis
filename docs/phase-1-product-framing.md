# Phase 1 – Product Framing

## Problem Statement
India’s fast‑growing fashion e‑commerce market sees **30‑40 %** of apparel returns driven by size/fit mismatches. Returns are costly (logistics, reverse‑shipping, carbon footprint) and erode shopper confidence.

## Personas
| Persona | Age | Shopping Behaviour | Pain Point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Priya** | 25 | Mobile‑first, buys trendy brands, limited measurement knowledge | Unsure which size to pick for new brands |
| **Meera** | 35 | Buys for family, price‑sensitive, compares multiple items | Returns due to “too small/large” across categories |
| **Ananya** | 22 | First‑time buyer, explores D2C launches | Lacks confidence without fit reviews |

## Core Hypotheses
1. **Proactive size guidance** displayed on the PDP reduces uncertainty and lowers the non‑fit rate.
2. **Personalised recommendations** based on height, weight, body‑type and historical fit feedback increase conversion without hurting add‑to‑bag rate.
3. A **visual size‑fit heatmap** for each category helps users self‑diagnose fit risk.

## Success Criteria
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Non‑fit (small + large) feedback rate on PDP | **≤ 22 %** (↓ 4 pp from baseline 26 %) |
| Add‑to‑Bag conversion (PDP → BAG) | **≥ 0 % impact** (guardrail) |
| Recommendation adoption (click‑through) | **≥ 15 %** |

---
*All numbers are derived from the Rent‑the‑Runway dataset (see `data/processed/fit_feedback.db`).*
